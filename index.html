FAQ / Suggested Questions

help How do I participate?

Just grab your bike, helmet, and start riding! Track your miles using Strava and upload them here.

account_circle Do I need a Strava account?

Yes, we use Strava to verify your rides. Ask a parent to help you set one up!

directions_bike What activities are eligible?

Any bicycle ride counts! Just make sure you're riding safely on designated paths.

screenshot What should my screenshot show?

Your screenshot must clearly show the activity date and distance (at least 1 mile!).

military_tech When will I receive my reward?

Badges are unlocked immediately after verification. Keep riding to level up!

error What happens if my submission is rejected?

We'll let you know why. Usually, it's just a missing date or distance.
